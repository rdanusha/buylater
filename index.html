How BuyLater worksNew here? Start with this quick guide.⌄

Put a little space between wanting something and buying it.

  1. Save what caught your eye.

    Enter an item name and price, with a product link if you have one. Choose your currency before adding your first item.

  2. Give it time.

    Choose 24 hours, 7 days or 30 days. Add it to your wishlist and let the countdown run.

  3. Come back and decide.

    When ready, choose Bought, Skipped or Still thinking for another 24 hours. Bought records your choice; this app does not buy anything for you.

  4. Keep your list safe.

    Items save automatically in this browser. Use Export backup to keep a copy. Import replaces your list after confirmation.

There are no background reminders, so return when you are ready. Explore demo below lets you try fictional items without changing your saved list. Skipped spending is not verified bank savings.

Privacy and important details

Your wishlist stays in this browser profile. It is not encrypted or synced between devices. Clearing site data can erase it, so export a backup. Local and hosted lists are separate; use export/import to transfer them.

We do not add analytics or send wishlist data to a server. GitHub Pages serves this website and may log normal web requests. Other applications hosted on this same github.io origin are not a separate browser-storage security boundary.

The waiting period follows your device clock and has no background notifications. Skipped spending is not verified bank savings. To remove personal entries, delete the items individually or clear this site's browser data. Imported backups replace your list after confirmation.
